Privacy Policy — ScreenBee 🐝

Version 1.2.7 · Last updated: September 2026

In short: your recordings never leave your computer. ScreenBee runs no servers and no analytics. The only personal data involved is an optional email address you may enter to start a free trial — and even that is stored in your own browser, not sent to us.

1. What ScreenBee stores, and where

DataWhere it livesWhy
Video & audio you record In browser memory while recording, then written as a .webm file to your Downloads folder This is the product. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Preferences (language, recording mode, quality, mic on/off) chrome.storage.local on this device So the extension opens the way you left it
Email address (optional) chrome.storage.local and chrome.storage.sync Only if you choose to start the 7-day PRO trial. Used to count trial days and prevent the same trial being restarted repeatedly. You can skip this and keep using the free plan.
License key, activation date, random device ID chrome.storage.local / chrome.storage.sync To keep a PRO purchase active on this device

About Chrome Sync: anything written to chrome.storage.sync is synchronised by Google across Chrome profiles you are signed into. That is a Chrome feature; ScreenBee has no access to Google's copy.

2. Network connections

ScreenBee makes exactly one kind of outbound request, and only when you ask it to:

  • When you activate or re-validate a PRO license key, the key is sent to Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com) to check whether it is valid. Re-validation happens at most once every 3 days.

There is no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking script, and no remotely hosted font or code. Your recordings are never transmitted.

3. Browser permissions and why each is needed

  • tabCapture — captures video and audio of the browser tab you chose to record.
  • activeTab + scripting — draws the countdown, the floating control bar and the webcam bubble on the page being recorded.
  • Camera & microphone (requested by the browser at the moment of use) — captures your webcam bubble and narration. Streams are processed in memory and released as soon as recording ends.
  • offscreen — runs MediaRecorder in a hidden document, as Manifest V3 requires.
  • storage — saves the preferences and license data listed in section 1.
  • downloads — writes the finished .webm file to your Downloads folder.
  • Access to all websites (optional, off by default) — you may grant this so the control bar survives when you navigate to another page mid-recording. ScreenBee never reads or transmits page content, and if you decline, recording still works normally.

4. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share user data with third parties. Apart from license validation, no data ever reaches us in the first place.

5. Retention and deletion

Recording data is discarded from memory once the file is written. To erase everything else, remove the extension at chrome://extensions, or use "Deactivate on this device" in the PRO panel to delete the stored license. Uninstalling clears local storage; synced values are removed by Chrome from your profile.

6. Children's privacy

ScreenBee is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. The only field a user can type into is an optional email address.
